1. Status of this website

peptid.co.uk is a public website. These pages describe the website. They do not describe goods for sale. Nothing on this website is an offer to sell or supply, and nothing here is an advertisement for a product.

2. No sale of medicines

This website does not sell unlicensed medicines. It does not sell any goods. There is no shop.

There is no price list, no basket, no checkout, and no trade account. You cannot place an order. A message to info@peptid.co.uk does not create an order.

If an older page, a search result, or a saved copy shows a shop at this domain, that is not the current website.

3. No product information

This website does not name, list, or describe products. It does not explain how to obtain, make, import, or use a substance. Questions that ask for that are outside what this website will answer.

4. Publisher and contact

The contact email for this website is info@peptid.co.uk.

This website does not publish a company registration number, a registered office, or a telephone number. Those details are omitted because they have not been confirmed for publication. Do not rely on a street address, a telephone number, or a company number shown elsewhere for this domain.

5. Use of these pages

You may read these pages. You may send a general question about the website to the published email.

These pages are about the website itself. They are not medical advice, and they are not advice about your own situation. They are not a statement that any authority has approved this website.

You may not use this website to try to buy goods. There is nothing for sale.

6. Email you send

If you write to info@peptid.co.uk, the message is ordinary email to that address. It may be read so that we can reply. The form on the contact page opens your own email program. It does not send the message to a server run by this website, and the website does not keep a copy.

Write only what you would put in ordinary email. Do not send payment card numbers. This website does not take payment.

We do not sell personal information. We do not run accounts. We do not add you to a mailing list. We do not pass your message to an advertising network.

You may ask, at the same email address, for messages you sent to be deleted. We will delete the messages we still hold in that mailbox.

If you want a reply, include an email address we can answer. A reply is not guaranteed.

7. Cookies

These pages do not use analytics cookies or advertising cookies. They do not embed third-party media. No cookie banner is shown, because the site does not set optional cookies.

8. Shipping and returns

This website does not dispatch goods. There is no delivery time and no returns process. An email is correspondence. It is not an order and it is not a delivery.

9. Changes to this notice

This notice was published on 1 October 2026. If the text changes, the date at the top of this page will change with it. The version published on this page is the current one.

End of notice.
